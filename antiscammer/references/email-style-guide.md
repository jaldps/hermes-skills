# Scam Email Replier — Style Guide

## The Core Principle
Take a single simple idea and膨胀 (inflate) it into thousands of words of impenetrable prose. The recipient should need 10+ minutes to read it and walk away understanding almost nothing new.

## The Three Personas

### 1. The Pretentious Professor
- Cite real and fake academic papers (e.g., "As established by Professor H.J. Mumblethwaite in his seminal 2019 treatise 'Epistemological Frameworks for Cross-Border Monetary Instruments'")
- Use academic hedging: "It may perhaps be reasonably ventured that..."
- Insert Latin: "mutatis mutandis", "ceteris paribus", "sui generis", "inter alia", "de novo", "a priori", "a posteriori", "in situ", "prima facie", "pro tanto"
- Define common terms in excruciating academic detail

### 2. The Corrupt Lawyer
- Excessive conditional clauses: "subject to", "notwithstanding", "without prejudice to", "insofar as"
- Fake legal citations: "pursuant to Section 4,817(b)(iii) of the International Transnational Fiscal Cooperation Accord (2017)"
- Definitions sections that define words nobody needs defined
- Each sentence should have at least 3 subordinate clauses
- Use "herein", "thereof", "wherein", "theretofore", "hereinafter"

### 3. The Government Bureaucrat
- Reference non-existent regulations and agencies
- Procedural tangents: explain the 12-step process for something that doesn't need a process
- Form numbers and reference codes: "Form ITF-2947/Rev.C", "Reference No. XB-7741-Q"
- "In accordance with", "pursuant to", "as per the provisions outlined in"
- Acknowledgments of receipt that take 300 words

## Structural Template

### Opening (200-300 words)
Acknowledge receipt of the communication in the most roundabout way possible. Reference the date, time (make up a timezone), and method of receipt. Establish your "authority" to respond.

### Definitions Section (300-500 words)
Define 5-8 terms from the scam email. For a "Nigerian prince" scam, define: "prince", "Nigeria", "transfer", "fund", "assistance", "confidentiality". Each definition should be 40-60 words and reference fake regulatory frameworks.

### Substantive Response (800-1200 words)
This is where you convey your ONE idea (usually "I'm interested, tell me more" or "Please provide additional details"). But it's wrapped in:
- 3 paragraphs of context about why this type of arrangement requires careful consideration
- 2 paragraphs citing (fake) precedents
- 1 paragraph of philosophical reflection on the nature of the transaction
- 2 paragraphs of conditional acceptance with so many caveats it's unclear whether you're agreeing
- 1 paragraph requesting more information but so vaguely worded they can't tell what you want

### Footnotes Section (200-400 words)
At least 5 footnotes. Each should be 30-80 words. Some should reference other footnotes. At least one should be entirely in Latin.

### Closing (150-250 words)
Take an extraordinarily long time to say "I look forward to hearing from you." Include your fake title, fake organization, and fake reference numbers.

## Word Choice Guidelines

### Instead of "yes" write:
"Subject to the foregoing provisions and without prejudice to any rights or remedies that may hereinafter accrue, it may be affirmatively stated that the proposition outlined in your communication finds itself not inconsistent with the parameters of my considered deliberation"

### Instead of "send me more info" write:
"It would be of considerable assistance to the furtherance of our mutual deliberations if you might see fit to transmit, at your earliest convenience, such additional particulars, specifications, and ancillary documentation as may be reasonably germane to the matters at hand"

### Instead of "I'll help you" write:
"Insofar as the modalities of the proposed arrangement permit, and contingent upon the satisfactory resolution of the preliminary considerations enumerated herein, I am disposed to entertain the possibility of extending such cooperation as may be deemed appropriate under the circumstances"

## Red Lines — NEVER:
- Send money or provide real financial information
- Include real personal details (address, phone, SSN, etc.)
- Click links or open attachments from the scam
- Agree to anything that could be interpreted as a binding commitment
- Use your real email signature or real company name
- Provide any information that could be used for identity theft

## Length Targets
- Minimum: 1,500 words
- Sweet spot: 2,000-3,000 words
- For special occasions: 4,000+ words

The longer the better — the entire point is to waste the scammer's time.
